# Integrated negative constraints

The built-in image tool did not expose a separate negative-prompt input. These constraints were integrated into each actual request:

- no toy plastic or handmade craft texture
- no oversized branding, real company/port/facility identity, flags or addresses
- no fake Chinese, operating metrics, qualifications or SLA claims
- no sci-fi holograms, neon-heavy treatment or cyan environmental wash
- no crowds, identifiable faces, unsafe work or dirty facilities
- no floating cargo, object intersections, cloned patterns, melted wheels/racks or implausible logistics
- no extreme bokeh, crushed shadows or aggressive lens distortion
- no in-image text, logos, signs, letters, numbers or watermark
